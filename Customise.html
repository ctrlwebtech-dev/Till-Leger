Topic 5: Modification of Information Systems

Design and Pedagogy of Reasoning

It is quite common for small businesses to fall into the trap of over-customizing an existing but inflexible software package or trying to develop a completely customized software solution on their own.

To make the process of adapting their systems easier for the owners, this topic was organized according to the System Development Life Cycle (SDLC).

Through the discussion of an API-based checklist, retail managers are shown how they can customize the existing POS and ecommerce platforms to match their own processes.
